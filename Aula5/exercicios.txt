A) Complete com o verbo correto:

* She **worked** yesterday.
* They **played** soccer last week.
* He **studied** English.
* I **fixed** the bug.

B) Transforme em negativa:

* She worked → **She didn't work.**
* He studied → **He didn't study.**
* They played → **They didn't play.**

C) Transforme em pergunta:

* You worked → **Did you work?**
* She studied → **Did she study?**
* They played → **Did they play?**

D) Corrija os erros:

* I did worked → **I did work.**
* She didn't studied → **She didn't study.**
* Did you worked? → **Did you work?**

E) Traduza:

* Eu trabalhei ontem → **I worked yesterday.**
* Nós corrigimos o erro → **We fixed the bug.**
* Ela estudou inglês → **She studied English.**
